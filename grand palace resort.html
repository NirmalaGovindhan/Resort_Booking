Grand Palace Resort

Where Royalty Meets Luxury in Yercaud

Grand Palace Resort in Yercaud stands as a pinnacle of regal luxury nestled amidst the verdant hills and mist of the Shevaroy ranges. Overlooking the serene Yercaud lake and lush valley vistas, our resort offers majestic architecture, world-class amenities, and impeccable hospitality designed to make every guest feel like royalty. From our opulent rooms with panoramic mountain views to our gourmet restaurant, scenic swimming pool, and rejuvenating Ayurvedic spa, every moment spent at Grand Palace is a celebration of peace, elegance, and comfort.

The Royal Feast: Gourmet Dining

Experience regal dining at our signature multi-cuisine restaurant. Featuring culinary masterpieces prepared by our master chefs using fresh, locally sourced ingredients. Savor delicious South Indian delicacies, continental classics, and authentic tandoori delights amidst ambient royal decor with panoramic mountain views.

Grand Events & Royal Celebrations

Host your destination weddings, family celebrations, and corporate retreats in our majestic banquet halls and open-air garden amphitheatre. With professional event coordination, state-of-the-art audiovisual setups, and exquisite catering, your special day will be nothing short of magnificent.

Palace Ayurvedic & Wellness Spa

Surrender your senses to rejuvenating wellness therapies inspired by traditional Ayurvedic rituals and modern spa techniques. Unwind with soothing herbal oil massages, body scrubs, and wellness packages crafted to detoxify, restore energy, and relax your mind amidst the tranquility of Yercaud.
