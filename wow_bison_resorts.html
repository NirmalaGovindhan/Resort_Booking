Your Tranquil Retreat in Yercaud

Experience nature, comfort and peaceful moments at our beautiful resort.Set amidst the tranquil hills of Yercaud, a destination celebrated for its lush landscapes and serene charm, our boutique hotel offers an idyllic retreat approximately 1 km from Peeku Park, Yercaud and about 2 km from Pagoda Point. Featuring a range of accommodations, including cosy rooms, spacious tents, and a charming bungalow, we cater to every traveller seeking comfort and tranquillity. host seamless events in our well-equipped banquet hall or unwind at our serene spa. With recreational facilities for all ages and a strong commitment to sustainability, our boutique hotel in Yercaud provides a harmonious blend of relaxation, convenience, and responsible hospitality, making it the ideal retreat amidst Yercaud's natural beauty.
