Sterling Resort

Where Luxury Meets Nature in Yercaud

Sterling Resort in Yercaud stands as an iconic destination that perfectly blends luxury with the pristine beauty of the Shevaroy Hills. Surrounded by lush coffee and orange plantations, our resort offers stunning views, world-class amenities, and personalised service that makes every guest feel special. From our beautifully appointed rooms to our gourmet restaurant and rejuvenating spa, every aspect of Sterling has been crafted to give you an unparalleled holiday experience. Discover the magic of Yercaud with Sterling as your home away from home.

Gourmet Dining at Its Finest

Our award-winning multi-cuisine restaurant serves a delightful array of Indian, Continental and Asian cuisines. Enjoy breakfast with misty hill views, romantic candlelight dinners, and themed buffets on special occasions. Our chefs use the freshest seasonal ingredients to create memorable dining experiences that are as beautiful as the surroundings.

Grand Events & Celebrations

Sterling's elegant event spaces can host everything from intimate anniversary dinners to grand destination weddings. Our dedicated events team will work with you to create a flawlessly executed event. With state-of-the-art audiovisual equipment, lush outdoor lawns, and exquisite banquet halls, your event will be truly spectacular.

Sterling Spa: Absolute Serenity

Surrender to the soothing touch of our expert therapists at Sterling Spa. Choose from an extensive menu of holistic treatments including hot stone massages, deep tissue therapy, aromatherapy, and Ayurvedic rituals. Our spa is designed to be a sanctuary of calm where the stresses of modern life simply melt away amidst the hills of Yercaud.
