Sunrays Inn Resort

A Sunny Escape in the Hills of Yercaud

Wake up to golden sunlight and breathtaking hill views at Sunrays Inn Resort, nestled in the serene hills of Yercaud. Our resort offers a warm, comfortable stay with modern amenities and nature's tranquility at your doorstep. Just minutes away from Yercaud Lake and Pagoda Point, we are the perfect base for your Yercaud getaway. Whether you're here to unwind, explore, or celebrate, our friendly staff and beautiful surroundings will make your stay truly memorable. Enjoy hearty meals, refreshing walks, and peaceful evenings under the stars.

Fresh & Flavourful Dining Experience

Savour delicious multi-cuisine meals prepared fresh daily using locally sourced ingredients. Our in-house restaurant serves an extensive breakfast buffet, hearty lunches, and romantic candlelight dinners amidst lush garden views. Whether you prefer South Indian comfort food or continental delights, our chefs craft every dish with love and care.

Celebrate Every Occasion with Us

From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, our event spaces are designed to make every occasion unforgettable. Our banquet hall accommodates up to 200 guests and is equipped with Wi-Fi, projector, sound system, and flexible seating. We offer complete event planning support for weddings, corporate events, birthday parties, and more.

Rejuvenate at Our Wellness Spa

Step into a world of relaxation at our serene spa. Choose from a wide range of rejuvenating therapies including Swedish massage, Ayurvedic treatments, and aromatherapy. Our expert therapists ensure a personalised wellness experience that leaves you feeling refreshed and energised. Spa packages are available for couples and groups.
